# Data minigames
A Wario ware style game, with a collection of minigames around the theme of data. You play through a series of minigames and try to maximise your score in a limited time!

Controls: mouse only
# Minigames

- Cats or dogs: you have to tell if a photo is showing a cat or a dog
- Different color: you have to click on the circle with a different color than the others
- Colored text: you have to click on the color described by the word, for example, if the word is "green" (but written in red), you have to click on green, and not red 
- Biggest produuct: you have to click on the biggest product, all of the products are made with numbers between 1 and 10
